The Method

Reading a word list isn't learning it.

Every session runs the same loop: pull the word out of memory, prove it four times, then let the schedule bring it back before it fades.

The Problem

Most vocab tools hand you a list and walk away.

The hard part isn't reading a definition once. It's getting the word to stick.

What's out there

  • Static word lists you memorize on your own.
  • Reinforcement left entirely to you.
  • The useful tools locked behind paywalls.
  • Bloated apps that bury the words under features.

What GRE Vocab Tester does

  • Active recall built in. Typos and your own wording still count.
  • No word is mastered until you get it right 4 times in a row.
  • Missed words return a few cards later. Mastered words return over weeks.
  • Completely free, no account. Export and import progress in Settings.
  • One job: getting GRE vocabulary to stick.
Why I built this

I couldn't pass the verbal section with unlimited time. A few weeks later I scored a 160 under real test conditions.

I had to take the GRE on short notice, and the math wasn't the problem. The vocabulary was. The verbal section leans on a few hundred high-frequency words, and if you don't know them, no strategy saves you.

The good resources were behind paywalls. The free ones were static lists that left the reinforcing to you. So I built the tool I wished existed, one that drills words through active recall until they stick, used it for my own exam, and kept it free for everyone else.
